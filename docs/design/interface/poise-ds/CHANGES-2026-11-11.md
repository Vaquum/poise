# What changed in Poise, 4 to 11 November

Read on 11 November from `main` at `bb30bb3`. The baseline is `741d2c4`, which [CHANGES-2026-11-04.md](CHANGES-2026-11-04.md) read. 136 pull requests merged. Most change the service, its evidence and its tests, and change nothing a person sees. Every interface file that changed was read, 21 of them, with the docs behind them, and so were the two pieces of work still in flight that change what a person sees.

## What shipped that a person sees

- **#853, Assign to Poise in Current.** An open issue shows whether it carries the chain's label, an *Assign to Poise* button that adds it on GitHub, and the open pull request that closes it, or *No open PR*. The chain does not take an issue that has one. `main` serves it as `assign_issue` and `issue_intake` on `/api/gh`, so the new interface needs no server change for it.
- **#1095 and #955, the chain's evidence.** It moved from Settings → General to the foot of Logging: who owns new work, the chain's model capacity, the pull requests v1 kept at cutover, every chain with Hold or Resume, and its journal. Notifications list held, unconfirmed and review-pending chain work at once, and any other after a day without a verified advance, with its gates, its next step and *Journal evidence*. `/?view=logging&chain=…&after=…` opens a journal at a step.
- **#1106, #1120 and #1119, work impact.** Each Logging row says what it did to managed work: retrying, blocked, recovered or unknown, with its target and stage. Its detail and a notice's detail say more: the last verified progress and the next retry or action.
- **#995, Analytics' distributions.** Under each year's weekly line, the 30 completed days before the chosen day, one value a day, in five bins. It was the year's 52 weeks in ten.
- **#857, #1081, #1085 and #1115, Swarm.** A chain's own GitHub action names what it did, such as *merge-if-ready*, and shows no model. Unreadable run records are counted once each and alert once.
- **Smaller.** Twelve new progress phases for chain runs, such as *Reading repair evidence*. A stale GitHub mirror says *GitHub mirror is stale. Behaviors will check again.*

## In flight, drawn now

- **#1101, System health.** A PRD the chain is building: five live cards over the last 24 hours at the top of Analytics. Errors per completed work item, work blocked, recovered by itself, time since the last work error, and evidence coverage.
- **#1133, Behaviors' metrics.** When unreadable run records leave coverage incomplete, the readable counts stay, marked partial, instead of every row going blank.

## Decisions

1. **The chain's evidence lives in Observe → Tasks, not at the foot of Logging.** Tasks already lists every task with its rail, timeline, Hold and Resume. Putting the evidence beside Logging's diagnostics would split one piece of work across two parts again. Logging keeps the diagnostics and gains Work impact, which links to the task.
2. **Assign to Poise follows `main`.** The owner's #849 asked to show a pull request and the chain being off, not to refuse them, so Assign stays offered and shows both. The ten-minute "not taken" message is gone: the closing pull request on the row says it first.
3. **System health sits under In motion.** It is live, as In motion is; the day's numbers below it follow the chosen day.

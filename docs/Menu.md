# Menu and Settings

The burger at the top right opens a surface attached to the button. Choose
Analytics, Logging, Extensions, or a destination under Settings: General, Navigation, Models, Accounts,
Publishing or Typography. Service administrators also see Admin. Analytics, Logging and
Extensions are views of their own: choosing one closes the menu and
replaces the page, and the menu marks it as the current page while it shows
(see [Analytics](Analytics.md), [Logging](Logging.md) and [Extensions](Extensions.md)). There is no second tab strip inside
Settings. Back returns to the menu on the same surface. The burger becomes
the single close button while the menu or a panel is open; clicking it or
pressing Escape closes the surface. Escape inside an account terminal belongs
to the running program.
If Writer mode is enabled with a panel open, the single close button remains
visible until the surface is dismissed.

Navigation chooses and arranges shortcuts above Settings in the menu and in
the top bar. Drag within either area to reorder, or into the other area to
move. Drag from the reusable All views palette below to add to either or both.
An existing destination shortcut is repositioned, without duplicates. The close
button removes only that area's shortcut. Changes reach actual navigation after
a successful drop; the insertion gap is a local preview. Dropping outside or
pressing Escape cancels. An unchanged drop does not write preferences.

Click or tap a shortcut to pick it up without holding, then click or tap its
insertion position to place it. All views also opens Add to top bar / Add to
menu buttons. With a keyboard, Space or Enter picks up a shortcut; Up/Down
arrange, Home/End reach the ends, Left chooses Top bar, Right chooses Menu,
and Space/Enter drops. Escape cancels and returns focus to the origin; Escape
while idle closes Settings. Tab cancels and continues traversal. Position and
completion are announced. Short panels scroll within Settings, including during
a held drag. Native touch movement is consumed during a held drag so the next
tap can activate normally; panning outside a drag remains available. Narrow
panels stack the two areas. External preference changes,
panel dismissal, resize and focus/typography changes discard an active move.

Changes apply immediately and stay in this browser, including across reloads
and other open tabs. Restore defaults restores the original visibility and
order: Analytics, Logging then Extensions in the menu; Current, Swarm, Chat, Archive,
Behaviors, Snippets then Editor in the top bar. A view added to Poise later
starts where its default places it, after your saved order. Settings and release information
remain accessible. Hiding the current view's shortcuts leaves that view open.
Selecting Analytics on either surface opens Today.

General holds GitHub identities, accounts to follow, time, notifications,
chat defaults and theme. Models holds named configurations and model choices.
Changes that need saving have a Save button at the bottom. Accounts separates
AI provider sign-ins from GitHub sign-ins, with Poise Link in service mode and
a workspace terminal. Publishing lists the websites the Editor publishes to
(see [Publishing](Publishing.md)). Typography controls fonts, spacing and density.

Explanations appear after half a second over the relevant label, heading,
value or action, and on keyboard focus. Errors, account state and pairing
instructions remain visible.

The frame follows the burger across views and viewport sizes. Reduced-motion
preferences remove its animations. Existing account, pairing and admin links
open their destination directly.
